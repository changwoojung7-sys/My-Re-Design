📌 1. Executive Summary

MyReDesign is an AI-powered lifestyle habit formation OS designed to help users build sustainable daily routines. Through personalized AI mission generation, multimedia proof tracking, growth analytics, history video recap, and 1:1 buddy challenges, users design and elevate their daily lives.

🛠️ 3. Core Feature Walkthrough

  1. AI Daily Missions (Today Tab): Every morning, AI creates 3 personalized missions based on current mood & wellness goals (Body, Mind, Growth, FunPlay).
  2. Multi-type Proofs: Users verify missions by capturing photos, recording videos/audio, or writing reflective text.
  3. PlayMovie (History Tab): Verified photos and videos are automatically transformed into a cinematic short-form recap reel.
  4. Growth Dashboard & AI Reflection: Streaks, completion rate charts, level milestones, and end-of-day AI coaching.
  5. 1:1 Buddy Challenge (Friends Tab): Challenge friends to custom missions and track real-time progress head-to-head.

🛡️ 4. Safety & Guideline Compliance

  • AI Safety (Guideline 1.2 / 5.1): Strict guardrails ensure all AI-generated content focuses exclusively on positive daily health and wellness routines.
  • Data Protection & Privacy: User media is stored in isolated cloud storage with strict Row Level Security (RLS).
  • Account Deletion (Guideline 5.1.1(v)): Users can delete their account and all data instantly via MyLoop ➡️ Account Settings ➡️ Delete Account.
